## 4. Live demo walkthrough

The examiner exercised the public demo at https://demo.flowhub.freaxnx01.ch first-hand. This is direct evidence folded into the two KI-bucket items above; it is **stronger** than the task brief assumed.

**Reachability / health.** `GET /` => 200, `GET /health/live` => 200 ("Healthy"). Home dashboard renders cleanly (banner, live-service chips, quick-capture bar, recent captures, skill/integration health). Integration health panel: Authentik / Paperless / Vikunja / Wallabag all *healthy*; skill health Articles/Belege/Books/Knowledge/Movies/Zitate all healthy.

**What was submitted & how it was classified (end-to-end, live):**
- **Capture 1** — read-later arxiv URL. POST returned stage=Raw; ~2s later already **stage=Completed**, matchedSkill=**Wallabag**, externalRef=17, AI-enriched title *"Attention Is All You Need Paper"* (correctly resolved arxiv 1706.03762 to the real paper title without fetching it). classifierTrace: kind=Ai, provider=OpenRouter, model=`google/gemma-4-31b-it:free`, promptTokens=319, completionTokens=58, latencyMs=3392. captureId 09cd4d0e-e369-44b2-8c5b-c33b15173bac.
- **Capture 2** — "todo: buy milk". Classified to matchedSkill=**Vikunja**, vikunjaProject=Inbox, externalRef=61, title "buy milk", **stage=Completed**, trace latencyMs=2535.

Two structurally different inputs (URL vs task) routed correctly to two different downstream skills by the same model -> flexible, intent-based routing, not brittle pattern matching.

**Embeddings / semantic search.** Deliberately disabled: `GET /api/v1/captures/search?q=paper` => **HTTP 503** with a clean RFC 7807 ProblemDetails body (type -> docs/problems/validation.md, title "Semantic search not available.", detail explains `Embeddings__ApiKey` unset, traceId included). Transparent and documented, not hidden.

**Rate limiting.** Present and effective under concurrency: 80 concurrent `GET /` => exactly 40x200 / 40x429; 40 rapid `POST /api/v1/captures` => interleaved 429s. It does NOT trip on slow sequential traffic (25 sequential GETs all 200) — burst/concurrency-sensitive, contrary to the brief's "200s rolling into 429s after ~25 sequential" expectation.

**Reset posture.** Banner states data resets every 15 min and semantic search is off; self-reset not directly observed in-session but documented transparently.

**Screenshots:**

**Issues observed (for accuracy / defense readiness):**
1. **Brief-vs-reality mismatch (in the demo's favour):** the documented posture says skill *writes are disabled* and final stage should be "Unhandled / no integration registered". Observed: both captures reached **Completed with live externalRefs** (Wallabag #17, Vikunja Inbox #61) -> the demo performs **live side-effecting writes**. Either docs/ADR should be updated, or confirm this is intended for a public endpoint.
2. **Public demo write-path = abuse surface:** anonymous POSTs create real downstream entries. The 15-min reset clears FlowHub's own DB, but it is unclear whether the Wallabag/Vikunja entries are also purged — otherwise the live tools accumulate junk.
3. **Rate-limit narrative:** any walkthrough claiming "200s -> 429s" must specify it needs *concurrent* load to reproduce.
4. **Self-inflicted pollution:** ~40 "rl test" orphan captures now dominate "Needs attention" (32 orphan) and the captures list — expected to clear at next reset, but a grader hitting the demo immediately after would see a cluttered view.
5. **Free-tier model risk:** `google/gemma-4-31b-it:free` can be upstream rate-limited/deprecated, silently degrading the live classification the rubric depends on. Consider a fallback model or a note.

1. UC-09's acceptance criterion says a Capture must "reach Completed", but your own flow and as-built code terminate at "Routed", and your notes admit there is no Completed transition. Is that acceptance criterion currently satisfiable, and which is the source of truth — the spec or the code?
2. NfA-P1/P2 are marked ZIEL / not-yet-implemented. Why are they in the SMART catalogue as requirements rather than backlog, and how would you verify them today?

**Entwurf**
3. Your canonical ER diagram shows `vector(1024)` with mistral-embed, but migration 0013 resized to `vector(384)` with multilingual-e5-small. Which is the as-built model, and how did the diagram drift?
4. ADR 0002 keeps a modular monolith over microservices on a PoC trade-off. At what scale or failure mode would you actually split, and what in the current design makes that split cheap?

**Programmierung**
5. Walk me through the deterministic fallback path in AiClassifier — what exactly triggers it, what does it guarantee never happens, and how is that asserted in a test?
6. You externalise configuration per 12-Factor. Show me how a provider switch (Anthropic -> OpenRouter -> Mistral) happens with zero code change, and where secrets live.

**Validierung**
7. Your "294 green" figure is the offline suite. How many are genuine unit tests vs component/integration, and what is the actual unit-level coverage of core domain logic incl. error cases?
8. The AI components are trait-gated and skipped by default. How do you gain confidence the *live* AI path still works before you ship — and what did `just smoke-prod` actually catch?

**KI, Sub-Systeme & Reflexion**
9. Name three decisions you deliberately did NOT delegate to the AI. For each: what was the call, why was it yours to make, and where is the commit/ADR that records it? (This is the literal level-7 gap.)
10. The public demo performs live writes into your real Vikunja/Wallabag. Is that intended? How are downstream side-effects bounded and reset, and what stops anonymous abuse?
11. Your live classifier runs on a free-tier OpenRouter model. What is your fallback if it is deprecated or rate-limited upstream, and how would a grader notice degradation?
12. The classifierTrace exposes model/tokens/cost/latency per capture. How do you use that operationally — and would you trust it as evidence the AI role is "intelligent and flexible" rather than a single lucky route?
