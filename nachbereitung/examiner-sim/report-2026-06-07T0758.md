## 2. Overall Result

### FINAL: **81 / 90**

**Grade band: Excellent (≈ 5.5 / 6.0; ~90% of in-scope points).**

FlowHub is a genuinely strong, evidence-anchored submission. Specification (use cases, vision), design (C4 + ER + six ADRs), programming structure (clean hexagonal sub-system split with real ports/adapters), and validation (acceptance-criteria catalog, layered test strategy, dated 171-pass results table) are all at or near the top of their scales, and the AI/Reflexion bucket — highest-weighted at 30 points — is a standout, with auditable per-slice generated-vs-handwritten metrics and a self-critical cross-block Fazit. A live end-to-end demo confirmed the AI services first-hand: a bare arXiv URL was enriched to the correct human title and routed to Wallabag, a movie note routed to Vikunja, with graceful documented fallback, active rate limiting, RFC 9457 error contracts, and live Prometheus metrics. The two real point losses are honest, structural ones: the NfA catalog is only partly SMART (one-third are plain design constraints), and the "Sub-Systeme als unabhängige Container" criterion is not met because FlowHub is consciously a single-process Modular Monolith (ADR 0002) whose only genuine first-party split is a migrations init-job.

## 5. Live Demo Walkthrough

**Reachability:** `GET /` → 200 (~270 ms, 63 KB HTML); `GET /health/live` → 200 "Healthy". TLS on real subdomain `demo.flowhub.freaxnx01.ch`, HTTP/2, Kestrel behind edge proxy. Real MudBlazor UI (dashboard "Needs attention", recent-captures grid with lifecycle badges, skill/integration health, filters + search).

**Submitted & classified (first-hand end-to-end):**
- arXiv URL `1706.03762` → Raw → Unhandled in ~3 s. `matchedSkill=Wallabag`, AI-enriched title **"Attention Is All You Need Paper"** (model recognized the Transformer paper from the bare id — genuine LLM enrichment, not a static lookup), `failureReason="no integration registered for skill Wallabag"` (documented demo fallback). Capture id `abd7dc29-095d-4dc2-a0e9-de463530e819`.
- Note "The Matrix is a great movie I want to watch again" → `matchedSkill=Vikunja`, title "Watch The Matrix again", `vikunjaProject=Inbox`. Flexible multi-skill routing demonstrated live.

**Rate limiting:** Active/well-behaved. 25 rapid GETs → 24×200 then 1×429; harder → HTTP/2 429 "Too Many Requests" with `Retry-After: 3`. ~24-request burst window.

**Embeddings / semantic search:** `GET /api/v1/captures/search?q=paper` → 503 with proper RFC 9457 ProblemDetails (type, title "Semantic search not available.", detail naming `Embeddings__ApiKey`, instance, traceId). Disabled transparently.

**Reset posture:** Banner rendered ("Data resets every 15 min; external skill-writes + search are disabled."). The 15-min reset was OBSERVED — two captures created 08:03:14/15 vanished (404) between poll t=2s and t=4s, list re-seeded with fixtures timestamped 07:57-08:03. Transparent and as documented.

**Observability:** `/metrics` live (kestrel_*, dotnet_gc_*, aspnetcore_memory_*), confirming OpenTelemetry in the running container.

**Screenshots:**
- `nachbereitung/examiner-sim/screenshots/home-latest.png`
- `nachbereitung/examiner-sim/screenshots/captures-list.png`

**Issues:**
1. `/health/ready` returns **404** in production though docs list it as the readiness probe — only `/health/live` is wired. Wire it or document the deliberate omission.
2. The 15-min reset can wipe a capture mid-interaction (observed within ~4 s) — add a "next reset in N min" countdown or a grace window.
3. 429 body is plain text, not RFC 9457 — inconsistent with the 503/404 ProblemDetails contract.
4. Skill-writes + embeddings disabled, so examiner-supplied captures cannot reach a true Routed/Completed state (only seed fixtures show it). Documented and intentional, but the live external-integration leg is asserted, not demonstrated, for new input.

## 7. Defense Questions

**Spezifikation**
1. Your NfA catalog mixes SMART quality requirements with plain design constraints (index strategy, data-volume). Which NfAs are genuinely SMART, and how do you measure NfA-P1 Personendaten-Residenz and NfA-P2 KI-Transparenz (AI Act Art. 50) in CI?
2. UC-09 (async classify-and-route) has an AC tied to Skills.ContractTests — walk me through a failure path and how the test asserts the fallback.

**Entwurf**
3. You claim Struktur/Verhalten/Interaktion coverage, yet there is no rendered sequence or state diagram. Show me the behavioral perspective — what triggers Orphan vs Unhandled?
4. Your §6.1 hero SVG renders mangled. Which single diagram is the authoritative architecture view, and why?
5. Justify the soft-FK vs hard-CASCADE choices — when does a delete cascade vs RESTRICT?

**Programmierung**
6. Inline XML-doc coverage is ~27%, and FlowHub.Telegram/Integrations are empty `.gitkeep` dirs not in the solution. Why are they shown as layers? Where is the line between "documented design" and "scaffolding"?
7. Show me a driving port and its adapter — how does FlowHub.Core stay free of EF Core, and what does the *Entity-vs-domain-POCO split (ADR 0005) buy you?

**Validierung**
8. Your table shows 171 passing tests as of 2026-05-12; Task-21 live external-integration evidence is deferred. What does CI actually prove today vs what is asserted, and how would you close that gap?
9. Why Testcontainers over EF InMemory for the 29 Persistence tests — give a query that passes InMemory but fails real Postgres.

**KI, Sub-Systeme & Reflexion**
10. Defend the single-process Modular Monolith against "unabhängig als Container verteilt und betrieben." Your only first-party split is a migrations init-job — why is that the right trade-off, and what changes if you need independent scaling of the AI classifier?
11. In the live demo a bare arXiv id was enriched to the correct title. How do you prevent a plausible-but-wrong hallucinated title, and which guardrails (temperature, token cap, schema validation, EventId 3010 fallback) actually fire on a bad response?
12. Your Fazit refutes the "fast typist" hypothesis. If the bottleneck moved from typing to spec+review, what concretely changed between Block 1 and Block 5 to match review cadence to the failure class?
